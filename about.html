Why Llywelyn Bren?

I started out as Lapin Vert Woodworks, a private joke between my partner and I.
But as the shavings have piled up and the business has taken shape, it feels right to give it a name with deeper roots.
So now I'm Llywelyn Bren Woodworks.

Here’s why:

  • Llywelyn is my first name.
  • Bren (or pren) means wood in Welsh. 🪵 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁷󠁬󠁳󠁿
    • Llywelyn Bren was a 13th-century Lord of Senghennydd - literally Llywelyn of the Woods. 🫅 🌳🌳🌳🌳
  • I’m originally from Senghennydd, a small mining village nestled in the same historic cantref. So yes, Llywelyn Bren and I are from the same area.

It’s a name that feels personal and rooted. Still the same craftsmanship - just with a story carved into it. 🪚

Dechreuais allan fel Lapin Vert Woodworks, jôc breifat rhwng fy mhartner a minnau. Ond gan fod y shavings wedi pentyrru ac mae'r busnes wedi siapio, mae'n teimlo'n iawn i roi enw iddo gyda gwreiddiau dyfnach.
Felly nawr fi yw Llywelyn Bren Woodworks.
Dyma pam:
  • Llywelyn yw fy enw cyntaf.
  • Siwr nad oes rhaid i mi esbonio ystyr y gair pren? 😜🪵
  • Roedd Llywelyn Bren yn Arglwydd Senghennydd o'r 13eg ganrif 🫅 🌳🌳🌳🌳
  • Rwy'n wreiddiol o Senghennydd, pentref mwyngloddio bach yn swatio yn yr un cantref hanesyddol. Felly ydy, mae Llywelyn Bren a minnau o'r un ardal.

Mae'n enw sy'n teimlo'n bersonol ac wedi'i wreiddio. Dal yr un grefftwaith - dim ond gyda stori wedi'i cherfio iddi. 🪚
